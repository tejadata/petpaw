# PetsPaw

> Find trusted dog breed guides, pet care advice, puppies, and pet products in India.

PetsPaw (https://petspaw.in) is India's comprehensive dog parenting platform. We help dog parents discover the right breeds, access trusted pet care advice, find puppies from verified vendors, and shop curated pet products — all in one place.

## Core Values

- Adoption-first advocacy
- Education over sales
- Ethical breeding support
- Lifetime commitment to responsible pet ownership

## Key Features

### Breed Explorer
Browse 29+ dog breeds with detailed profiles covering traits, temperament, exercise needs, grooming, and compatibility.
- Browse all breeds: https://petspaw.in/breeds
- Each breed has a dedicated page: https://petspaw.in/breeds/{breed-slug}

### Breed Quiz
A 14-question interactive quiz (~5 minutes) that recommends compatible dog breeds based on your lifestyle, living space, budget, experience, and preferences.
- Take the quiz: https://petspaw.in/quiz

### Breed Comparison
Compare up to 3 dog breeds side by side — traits, exercise needs, grooming, and compatibility metrics.
- Compare breeds: https://petspaw.in/compare

### Marketplace
Browse puppies for sale and pet products from verified vendors across India, with category filtering.
- Visit marketplace: https://petspaw.in/marketplace

### Pet Care Hub
Combined resource center with health articles organized by category and homemade food recipes by diet type and life stage.
- Pet care hub: https://petspaw.in/pet-care
